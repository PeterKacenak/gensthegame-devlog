O hreAbout

GENŠ je first-person hra z prostredia slovenskej dediny na prelome tisícročí. Cieľom hry je sprostredkovať krutú a niekedy aj absurdnú realitu každodenného života v malej dedinke na Gemeri.GENŠ is a first-person game set in a Slovak village around the turn of the millennium. Its goal is to convey the harsh, and sometimes absurd, reality of everyday life in a small village in the Gemer region.
